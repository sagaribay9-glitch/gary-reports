The classic, tightened up

Everyone knows the shapes. We put the work into how they feel — pixel-crisp blocks, a smooth glide-and-lock so pieces settle instead of snapping, and screen shake that decays like it should.

The Korobeiniki theme is rebuilt as one clean looping track — no clicks between notes, no drift, just the tune the way it lives in your head.

What's in it

Sharp pixels

Blocks and grid snapped to exact pixel boundaries. No mush, no blur.

Smooth motion

Pieces glide toward their cell and settle; hard-drop and hold stay instant.

Harmonized palette

An evenly-spaced piece color palette on a gradient background, tuned for depth.

The theme, rebuilt

Korobeiniki mixed into one seamless loop — melody and bass, no jitter.
