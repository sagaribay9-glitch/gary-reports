The studio

WHO WE ARE

Jcorp Studios is a small independent shop. We build games the slow way: by hand, one honest slice at a time. No asset flips, no filler systems padding a menu. Every enemy, screen, and sound earns its place or it gets cut.

We ship in the open. Each build gets written up in the devblog with what changed and why.

How we build, and where AI fits

We use modern AI tools as part of how we make games: art help, code help, fast iteration. Every output is directed, reviewed, and hand-tuned before it ships. Taste, design decisions, and the quality bar stay human. Nothing goes in unless it stands on its own.

We say this plainly because you deserve to know how the thing in your hands was made. Honesty about the process is part of the craft.
