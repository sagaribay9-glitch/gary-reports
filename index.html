The studio

WHO WE ARE

Jcorp Studios is a small independent shop that builds games the slow way — by hand, one honest slice at a time. No asset flips, no filler systems padding a menu. Every enemy, every screen, every sound earns its place or it gets cut.

We ship in the open: each build gets written up in the devblog with what changed and why, so the work speaks for itself.

How we build — and where AI fits

We use modern AI tools as part of how we make our games — helping with art, code, and fast iteration. Every one of those outputs is directed, reviewed, and hand-tuned by us before it ships; the taste, the design decisions, and the quality bar are human, and nothing goes in unless it's good enough to stand on its own.

We're telling you this plainly because we think you deserve to know how the thing in your hands was made. Honesty about the process is part of the craft.
